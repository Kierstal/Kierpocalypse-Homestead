/*
 * Kierpocalyptic Homestead - food spoil-timer overrides
 *
 * Vanilla B42 ships some food items with absurdly short DaysFresh /
 * DaysTotallyRotten values:
 *   - 10 sealed canned vegetables at 30/60d (real cans are 2-5+ years)
 *   - Apple at 5/8d (real apples 2 weeks room temp, 1-2 months refrigerated)
 *   - Cabbage at 2/4d (cabbage keeps 1-3 weeks)
 *   - Bacon family at 3/5d (cured bacon, raw, lasts 1-2 weeks unsealed)
 *   - EggBoiled at 3/6d (hard-boiled in shell lasts ~1 week)
 *
 * Full vanilla item bodies are reproduced here (with only DaysFresh /
 * DaysTotallyRotten changed) because B42 item-script partial overrides
 * are unreliable - same merge issue that bit us with recipes.
 *
 * Skipped intentionally: anything where vanilla's value is already
 * defensible for an apocalypse with no fridge (bread, fresh meat,
 * mushrooms, leafy greens, prepared dishes, cooked egg dishes).
 */
